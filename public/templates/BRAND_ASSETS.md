The [MDIO brand-assets collection](https://library.rsamdio.org/) identifies its
Rotaract Cranberry Simplified artwork as an official logo resource for club
communications. The Rotary image is the simplified masterbrand provided by the
Brand Center. Both correspond to the current simplified signatures described in
the [official logo guidance](https://brandcenter.rotary.org/en-us/our-brand/brand-elements/logos-and-graphics/rotary-logos).

The renderer preserves aspect ratio, colors, whole artwork and surrounding space.
An uploaded club logo takes precedence over the optional template mark. The
template mark shows the configured club name separately; it does not generate or
claim to be a Brand Center club signature. Existing blocks without a template mark
retain their text identity. Template examples are illustrative, not claims of
Rotary International organization, endorsement or approval.

For a club's final identity, upload its actual club signature made with the
[Brand Center club templates](https://brandcenter.rotary.org/en-us/our-brand/brand-elements/logos-and-graphics/logos-for-clubs-districts-and-zones).
Follow the [official usage guidance](https://brandcenter.rotary.org/en-us/our-brand/brand-elements/logos-and-graphics/dos-and-donts),
including club identification and clear space. Do not crop the wheel out as an
icon, recolor the marks, or substitute the retired Rotaract seal.
